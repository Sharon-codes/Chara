2. Subgroup Selection Bug: Verification & Impact Diff

Diagnostic Bug Root Cause: In code/02_correct_diagnostics.py, development variance was loaded by querying key "targets", whereas distance arrays store target data under key "Y". The list remained empty and silently fell back to np.var(Y_true, axis=0) from the held-out test run! Consequently, the purported development-selected top quartile of variance was contaminated by held-out test data in all 12 folds.

Correction: Development arrays are now loaded strictly from distances_{r}.npz["Y"] for the two training runs assigned to that fold (Fold 1: reps 1+2; Fold 2: reps 1+3; Fold 3: reps 2+3), concatenating all development frames and selecting the top quartile of pooled development variance without test data fallback.

3. Exact Population-Moment Decomposition & Reference Values

For every target $j$ and model prediction, the temporal mean squared error is decomposed into squared temporal mean residual (Bias²) and residual variance:

MSE = (mean(y_pred - y_true))^2 + Var(y_pred - y_true) = Bias^2 + Var_res

Numerical checks verify that abs(MSE - (Bias^2 + Var_res)) < 1e-15 across all 528 diagnostic records.
