BIOPHYSICAL CONTEXT & METHODOLOGY

The Research Question: Sparse Distance Reconstruction

IIT Mandi Research Focus

1. The Biophysical Challenge of Trajectory Sampling

Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations generate high-dimensional conformational ensembles capturing macromolecular thermal fluctuations. Characterizing conformational dynamics across thousands of atomistic coordinates is computationally intensive.

A central biophysical question is whether measuring a small subset of residue-pair distances is sufficient to reconstruct the broader distance matrix in an independent simulation run of the same protein under identical conditions.

2. Multi-Run Transfer vs In-Sample Overfitting

Selecting sensor measurements requires rigorous out-of-sample discipline: Select measurements using transfer between development runs; evaluate once on the held-out run. Positive reconstruction skill indicates improvement over the development-mean baseline, rather than proof of an important biological mechanism.

CHARA evaluates transfer selection by using two development runs to select informative distances that minimize transfer prediction error onto an unseen third held-out run, measuring genuine out-of-run generalization.

Chara Trajectory Distance Reconstruction Pipeline

STAGE 1

Extract residue-pair distances from MD trajectories

Extracts pairwise inter-residue Cα distances dij(t) = ||ri(t) - rj(t)||2 with sequence separation |i - j| ≥ 3.

STAGE 2

Select informative measurements using two development runs

Applies greedy transfer search across two independent development runs to pick sensor distances, comparing M8 worst-direction and M7 mean-direction transfer.

STAGE 3

Fit a distance-reconstruction model on development data

Fits a multi-output ridge regression model Ŷ = (X - μx)W + μy, tuning shrinkage penalty λ across development runs.

STAGE 4

Evaluate reconstruction in a third, held-out run

Evaluates reconstruction on an independent third run using non-dimensional skill Skill = 1 - SSE / SSEbase relative to the development mean.

This does not prove equivalence. Variation among frames must not be used as uncertainty among proteins. Complete refit disclosed a near-tied regularization-choice mismatch between numerical solvers.

What is Retrospective Test-Centered Diagnostic Skill (Corrected)?

Post-Hoc Diagnostic Only

The raw archive's initial centered metric was uncorrected. Correct calculation centers each target time series and each predicted time series by its OWN temporal mean before calculating residual SSE: Skillcentered = 1 - [ SSE(Y - Ȳ, Ŷ - Ŷ̄) / SSE(Y - Ȳ, 0) ].

• M6 Pooled Greedy: 0.375201 (~0.375)
• M7 Mean Transfer: 0.356069 (~0.356)
• M8 Worst-Dir Transfer: 0.354314 (~0.354)

Note: Retrospective centering uses temporal means as an analytical diagnostic of dynamic fluctuation recovery; it is not a deployable inference input.

RESEARCH JOURNEY & SYSTEM AUDITS

Case Studies & Scientific Progression

Documenting the transition from early local simulation leads to standardized multi-protein external benchmarks, structural mechanism validations, and exploratory monitoring inquiries.

Origin Scope Definition

1. Origin & Research Scope Refocus

CHARA originated as an inquiry into macromolecular conformational transitions. Early hypotheses regarding clinical binding affinity were refocused following rigorous methodological review.

Defined Scope: Models reconstruct selected intramolecular distances in an independent run of the SAME protein under identical thermodynamic conditions. They do not claim drug binding, clinical benefit, or future-time forecasting.

Trajectory Reconstruction Scope Locked
Local Complex Lead cMYC Complex (Chain E)

2. The cMYC Complex Reconstruction Analysis

Analysis of chain E extracted from the simulated cMYC-containing complex. Initial exploration yielded a retrospective test-centered diagnostic skill of approximately +0.207, with 63.05% of MSE improvement arising from variance error reduction rather than static mean offset recovery alone.

Simulation Caveats: Coarse-grained MARTINI simulation with 349 elastic network restraints plus 10 backbone bonds. The analyzed chain was a subset extracted from the simulated assembly, and the monomer was not simulated alone.

349 Elastic Links + 10 Bonds Caveats Documented
Historical Milestone ATLAS Triplicates

3. Three-Protein External Pilot

To eliminate custom simulation artifacts, CHARA evaluated independent, standardized all-atom simulations from the ATLAS database across 3 proteins (1utg_A, 2cg7_A, 2j6b_A).

Pilot Result: Across 9 held-out runs simulated in explicit-solvent CHARMM36m, M8 maintained positive reconstruction skill (macro mean 0.370) without elastic network links.

9 Held-Out Folds Pilot Report
Completed Benchmark 36 Held-Out Folds

4. Twelve-Protein Benchmark Execution

Executed across 12 diverse proteins from the ATLAS repository with 3 independent run rotations each (36 evaluations total, k=10 sensors, 500 targets/fold).

Verified Results: M6 pooled greedy achieved mean skill 0.450 (worst-run 0.330), M7 mean transfer achieved 0.436 (worst-run 0.304), and M8 worst-direction transfer achieved 0.433 (worst-run 0.302).

Full Panel Verified 12P Report
Exploratory investigation · Novelty not established Equal Budget In-Progress

5. Can additional measurements reveal missed structural changes?

We are testing whether a few additional residue-distance measurements can flag reconstruction failures that ordinary uncertainty scores miss.

The planned comparison evaluates whether reserving a small portion of the measurement budget for monitoring (e.g., 10 reconstruction + 3 monitoring distances) provides practical value compared to full 13-distance reconstruction under an equal total measurement budget.

Note: Novelty has not been established. Earlier exploratory 5% error reduction figures are not presented as an established new-method result, and this remains an active exploratory inquiry.
