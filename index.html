THEORETICAL FOUNDATION

Problem vs. Innovation

Addressing cross-platform transcriptomic domain shift without compromising clinical model integrity.

The Problem: Severe Domain Shift

High-throughput RNA sequencing datasets exhibit dramatic platform-dependent variance. Differences in read depth, library preparation protocols, and sequencing hardware cause severe baseline shifts.

When a survival signature trained on TCGA (Illumina HiSeq) is evaluated directly on a local hospital cohort (Affymetrix or NovaSeq), baseline hazard estimates degrade, dropping predictive C-indices from 0.78 down to random guessing (< 0.52).

Data Leakage Flaw: Traditional batch correction methods (ComBat, MNN, Quantile Normalization) require pooling test samples with training samples. This violates clinical deployment rules by leaking test distribution statistics into model boundaries.

The Solution: Graph Laplacian Thermodynamics

Chara models patient transcriptomic expression profiles as vertices on a high-dimensional thermodynamic manifold. We construct a normalized Graph Laplacian matrix:

L = I - D^(-1/2) W D^(-1/2), where W_ij = exp(- ||x_i - x_j||^2 / (2 * sigma^2))

By solving heat kernel diffusion equations H_t = exp(-t * L), Chara maps the new cohort expression topology onto the frozen 4,337-gene Cox Proportional Hazards signature in one deterministic transformation—requiring zero retraining and zero data leakage.

PRACTICAL APPLICATIONS

Clinical & Computational Workflows

Bridging the gap between raw sequencing and actionable survival stratification.

1. Clinical Trial Cohort Stratification

Classify newly sequenced patient cohorts into high-risk vs. low-risk survival arms instantly, enabling targeted therapeutic trial designs without batch bias.

2. Cross-Platform Validation

Validate biomarker signatures developed on RNA-Seq against historical Microarray datasets or novel Nanopore long-read sequencing runs.

3. PyTorch / Scanpy Integration

Incorporate Chara manifold alignment as a pre-processing module inside deep survival networks or single-cell pseudo-bulk inference pipelines.
